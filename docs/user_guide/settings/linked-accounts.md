# Linked Accounts

Linked Accounts, under **Workspace** in the Settings navigation, is the one
genuinely per-user page in Settings. Everywhere else in Settings is
instance-wide and shared by every operator who signs in, but this page
manages *your own* signed-in identity. It's a single-section page, so
there's no "On this page" rail.

## Common tasks

### Link an external account

1. Go to **Settings → Linked Accounts**.
2. Click **Link Another Account**.
3. Choose the provider and complete its sign-in flow.

**Result:** The linked provider appears in your account list. Which
providers are available depends on what your admin has enabled under
[Authentication](authentication.md) (Local, Dispatcharr SSO, or others your
instance supports).

## Going deeper

- [Authentication](authentication.md) *(admin only)*: which sign-in providers are enabled instance-wide.
- [User Management](user-management.md) *(admin only)*: managing other users' accounts, as distinct from your own linked identities here.
